# Code of conduct

## Participation

Everyone is welcome to participate in portable-dataclass-mpy, regardless of
background, identity, experience, or ability. Treat contributors and users with
respect, and keep discussions focused on the project.

- Give constructive feedback and explain technical disagreements with evidence.
- Credit other people's work and respect their authorship.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to corrections and different experiences.
- Respect privacy, personal boundaries, and requests to stop unwanted contact.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexual harassment, deliberate personal
attacks, and sharing someone's private information without permission are not
acceptable. Disagreement and criticism of technical decisions are welcome when
expressed respectfully.

## Scope

These expectations apply to the project's issues, pull requests, discussions, and
other project-managed community spaces, and when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to [support@404builds.com](mailto:support@404builds.com).
Include relevant links, context, and any immediate concerns. Reports will be handled
with discretion; information may need to be shared to investigate or address an
incident.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, issue warnings, restrict
participation, or ban participants, depending on severity and context. Maintainers
are expected to follow the same standards and explain moderation decisions to the
people involved where practical. Questions or requests for reconsideration can be
sent to the same address.
